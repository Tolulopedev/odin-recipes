Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and herbs.

It is hearty, flavorful, and perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • Italian seasoning
Steps
  1. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instruction.
  2. Brown the ground beef and mix it with the tomato sauce.
  3. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  4. Repeat the layers untill all ingredients are used.
  5. Bake in a preheated oven untill the cheese is melted and golden.
  6. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving.
