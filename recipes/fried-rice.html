Fried-rice Recipe

Description

Fried rice is a flavorable dish made by stir-frying cooked rice with vegetables, eggs, and your choice of meat or seafood.

It is a quick and satisfying meal that is popular around the world and can be prepared with simple ingredients.

Ingredients
  • Cooked rice
  • Vegetales oil
  • Eggs
  • Carrots
  • Green peas
  • Sweet corn
  • Soy sauce
  • Cooked chicken
  • Spring onions
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large frying pan or wok.
  2. Scramble the eggs and set them aside
  3. Cook the carrots, peas, and corn until tender.
  4. Add the cooked rice and stir well.
  5. Mix in the chicken and scrambled eggs.
  6. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper then stir until everything is well combined.
  7. Garnish with chopped spring onions and serve warm.
