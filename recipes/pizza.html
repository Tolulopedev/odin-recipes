Pizza Recipe

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a baked dough crust topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of delicious toppings.

It is easy to customize with your favorite ingredients and is perfect for family meals, parties, or a quick dinner.

Ingredients
  • Pizza dough
  • Pepperoni slices
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Bell peppers
  • Onions
  • Olive oil
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 220%c (425%f).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a baking tray.
  3. Spread the tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Sprinkle mozzarella cheese on top.
  5. Add peperoni and your preferred vegetables.
  6. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
  7. Slice and serve while hot.
